--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: "How is KOSHA different from Khatabook, OkCredit, or Zoho Books?"
A1: "Zoho/Tally look BACKWARD at historical data for accounting and tax filing.

     KOSHA looks FORWARD: It predicts 90-day liquidity, stress-tests buyer delays,
     automates MSMED 19.5% penal interest, and links counter UPI repayments
     directly into rolling cash forecasting."

Q2: "Where does the forecast data come from, and how do you prevent duplicate entries?"
A2: "KOSHA ingests multi-bank CSV statements. Our Idempotent Deduplication Engine
     hashes files via SHA-256 and uses multi-attribute composite keys:
     (Date + Amount + UTR + Balance). Re-uploading the same statement safely skips
     duplicates without corrupting balances."
